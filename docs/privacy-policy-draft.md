# ehdate — Privacy Policy publication checklist (DRAFT; not legal advice)

Status: NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION. Confirm details with the operator and Canadian privacy counsel before publishing.

## Information to confirm
- Legal entity operating ehdate, mailing address, and privacy contact email.
- Data controller/service provider identities, hosting locations, cross-border processing and subprocessors.
- Exact analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs and cookies in website and upcoming iOS app.
- Retention schedule for accounts, chat, reports, backups, audit logs, verification photos, and support requests.
- Procedures for privacy access, correction, withdrawal of consent, complaints, and deletion requests.
- Consent and disclosure for approximate location, photos, public profiles, and marketing notifications.
- Whether biometric identification is used (current selfie review does not by itself establish automated biometric processing).
- How payment data will be handled once subscriptions are implemented.

## Required policy sections
1. Who operates ehdate and how to contact the privacy officer.
2. Eligibility (18+).
3. Information collected: account email, profile details, DOB, photos, preferences, approximate location when shared, messages, reports, verification submissions, usage/device data if collected.
4. Purposes and legal bases/consent; what is visible to other signed-in members.
5. Sharing: matched users, moderators, hosting and operational processors, lawful requests.
6. Storage and cross-border transfers, including Canada-region Supabase and any other service.
7. Retention and deletion, including evidence and backup exceptions.
8. User choices, privacy controls, access and correction requests.
9. Safeguards and limitations.
10. Children and age-restricted access.
11. Changes and effective date.

Do not claim all data is stored exclusively in Canada without verifying every processor, including authentication, email, logs, and analytics.
